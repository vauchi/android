Exchange contact cards in person via QR code. Update your info anytime —
contacts stay current automatically.

Features:
• End-to-end encrypted — only you and your contacts can read card data
• Decentralized — no central server owns your contacts
• Updatable — change your phone number or email and contacts get the update
• In-person exchange — scan a QR code face-to-face, no phone numbers needed
• Multi-device — sync across phone, tablet, and desktop
• No account required — your identity lives on your device

Vauchi works without Google Play Services. Sync uses background pull,
not push notifications. Fully functional on de-Googled Android
(GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, LineageOS, /e/OS).

Source code: https://gitlab.com/vauchi
